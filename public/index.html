Cleaner Air at Home

Small changes that reduce indoor particles

Filter one room

Run a room-sized HEPA purifier where your household spends the most time. HEPA filters capture fine airborne particles.

Time your ventilation

Keep windows closed when outdoor particle pollution is high. Air out your home when conditions improve.

Cut indoor smoke

Skip candles and incense on smog days. Use an outdoor-vented exhaust fan when cooking to remove cooking fumes.
